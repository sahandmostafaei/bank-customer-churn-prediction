# Project Documentation

## Bank Customer Churn Prediction

### 1. Objective

The objective of this project is to develop a supervised machine learning workflow for predicting customer churn in a banking dataset.

The project compares multiple classification algorithms and evaluates their ability to identify customers who are likely to leave the bank.

---

## 2. Business Problem

Customer retention is an important consideration for financial institutions.

A churn prediction model can help identify customers with a higher likelihood of leaving, allowing a bank to prioritize retention activities and investigate the factors associated with customer attrition.

The model is intended to support analytical decision-making rather than replace business judgment.
